We are looking for a motivated student to support our tool development and the integration of hardware designs for fault-injection simulation.

Properly functioning hardware relies on the correct processing of every bit. We create tools to investigate how hardware behaves under external influences such as radiation, aging, or targeted attacks. Since hardware designs have an extremely high number of possible states, we work on fast simulations that identify problematic designs before manufacturing.

In the [ISOLDE](https://aemy.cs.hm.edu/projects/isolde) project, we have already extended the open-source simulator Verilator to simulate such faults. To improve this solution and continue our research, we offer tasks in the following areas:

- Integration of open-source designs like RISC-V cores or crypto cores in our simulation flow
- Improving our fault-simulation framework
- Optimizing the fault-injection simulation inside Verilator
- Visualization and analysis of research results

Advantages of joining our research group:
- Insight into current research and hands-on work with open-source tools such as Verilator
- Room to bring in your own ideas
- A good stepping stone toward a thesis with us

Join us if you are motivated. You do not need prior knowledge of the specific topic, and we will discuss everything else in person. If one of these topics sounds interesting to you, or if you have an idea for a related topic, feel free to reach out!
