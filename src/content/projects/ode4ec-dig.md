Our team is a partner in the research project *ODE4EC-DIG: Digital SoC Design*,
coordinated by Politecnico di Milano (BMFTR grant 16MEE1153K). It is part of the *Open Design Environment for European Chips*
([ODE4EC](https://ode4ec.eu/)) initiative, which advances open source tools for
chip design so that European companies, startups and universities depend less
on proprietary tools. The project is funded by the European Union through the
Chips Joint Undertaking (Horizon Europe) and by the German Federal Ministry of
Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR), and runs from June 2026 to May 2029.

ODE4EC-DIG builds open source tools for designing digital systems-on-chip, from
design entry to physical integration, and validates them on real tape-outs.

HM-AEMY contributes to two tools:

- The open source chip design flow
  [LibreLane](https://github.com/librelane/librelane), with incremental builds,
  better reporting and improved stability
- The [CIRCT framework](https://circt.llvm.org), with inference of arithmetic
  and DSP blocks

We also lead the project's tasks on coordination across work streams,
communication and dissemination, and community building.

Read about the first ODE4EC meeting in our post
[ODE4EC Meeting September 2026](/2026/10/14/ODE4EC-2026.html).
