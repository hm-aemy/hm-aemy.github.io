For our research project [DI-OSVISE](https://aemy.cs.hm.edu/projects/osvise), we
are looking for a motivated student to support our work on open-source tools for
the chip design process.

Within the project we use a new and modern open-source compiler specifically for
chip designs.
Based on the LLVM project MLIR, [CIRCT](https://circt.llvm.org/) is actively
developed and shapes future design flows.
This allows us to establish new connections between existing tools as well as
creating new functionalities.

Your work can focus on one specific topic within this area.
For example, this can be for optimization, analysis, simulation, or synthesis.
While the tools are for silicon hardware, the job mostly requires expertise in
software development, so no prior knowledge of chip design is needed.

As CIRCT uses MLIR, and this in turn is inspired by LLVM, one of the most
important compiler projects and widely recognized in industry, the experience
gained will not only be helpful for hardware roles, but for compiler development
in general.

As the topic is not fixed, please feel free to reach out directly so we can
discuss a potential topic for you to work on.
