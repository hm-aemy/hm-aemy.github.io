We offer a full-time position (E13 TV-L, fixed-term for two years, PhD possible)
in our team for current and upcoming European research projects in the area of
open source chip design tools. Reference number: 07-06-26. Application deadline:
December 3, 2026.

Your tasks include:

- Research and development of open source tools for the design of digital circuits and systems-on-chip, e.g. hardware description languages, compiler infrastructure (CIRCT/MLIR), synthesis or simulation
- Development of methods and tools for verification and test, e.g. portable stimulus, cocotb or formal verification
- Integration of the tools into end-to-end design flows and validation on open source RISC-V systems
- Collaboration in European research projects with partners from academia and industry and in open source communities, and publication of results

You are the excellent candidate if you fulfill the following requirements:

- Completed Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical and Information Engineering, or a comparable field of study
- Knowledge of digital circuit design or verification, or the interest to develop your skills in this field
- Experience in software engineering, e.g. in Python, C++ or Rust
- Ideally experience in contributing to open source projects
