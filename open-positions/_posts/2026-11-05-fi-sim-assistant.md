We are looking for a motivated student to assist in different topics regarding tool developent and design integration.

Hardware must respond correctly even when individual bits flip, e.g. through radiation, aging, or targeted attacks. 
Whether such a fault will have no consequences, be detected, or go unnoticed while producing incorrect results can be determined even before manufacturing begins through fault-injection simulation.

Therefore we started investigating possibilities to simulate fault injection with the open-source simulator Verilator in our research group.
In the context of the [ISOLDE](https://aemy.cs.hm.edu/projects/isolde) project we already implemented a first iteration of a solution. To further improve the implemented solution and continue researching the topic of fault-injection we offer tasks in the following topics:
- Integration of open-source designs like RISC-V cores or crypto cores in our simulation flow
- Coding projects to further develop our fault-simulation framework
- Optimization of the fault-injection simulation inside Verilator
- Visualization and analysis of research results

In return, we offer:
- Insight into current research and hands-on work with open-source tools such as Verilator
- Room to bring in your own ideas
- A good stepping stone toward a thesis with us

In generall no specific prior knowledge is required.
Motivation is what counts most for us, and we’ll discuss everything else in person.
If one of these topics sounds interesting to you, or if you’ve been inspired and have a suggestion for a related topic, feel free to reach out!
