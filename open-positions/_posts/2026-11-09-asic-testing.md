In our research group, we design and test custom integrated circuits. The first two iterations of our custom RISC-V System-On-Chip (SoC) are back from fabrication and ready to be tested. We are looking for a motivated student to help out in setting up a test platform and executing bring-up tests for our chips. 

Hatch 1.0 and 2.0 are the very first chip designs of our chip development platform, with more iterations to follow. The first two iterations consist of

- a 4-stage RISC-V CPU,
- a tightly coupled WASM accelerator,
- a true random number generator (TRNG),
- 16 kB of internal SRAM,
- and some general-purpose peripherals: UART, GPIO and JTAG.

The work consists of the following tasks:

1. PCB design for a chip-on-board (COB) platform.
2. Collaboration with our bonding lab to bond the bare die onto the COB board.
3. Chip bring-up and electrical testing.
4. Verification of all functionalities, including the accelerator and the TRNG.

Requirements for this position
- Hands-on experience working with tools like oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.
- Experience programming microcontrollers in C.
- Interest in digital design and embedded systems.

Nice to have
- First experience with PCB design.
- Ability to read and write HDL code (SystemVerilog).
- Experience with interfaces like UART, SPI and JTAG.
- Basic knowledge of SoC components and how they interact.

If this position sounds interesting to you and you want to learn more about the world of chip design and testing, feel free to reach out to us!
