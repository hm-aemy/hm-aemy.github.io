In our research group, we design and test custom integrated circuits, and the first two iterations of our custom RISCV System-On-Chip (SoC) are back from fabrication and ready to be tested. We are looking for a motivated student to help out in this task. 

Hatch-1.0 and -2.0 are the very first chips design and ...

- A 4-stage RISCV CPU.
- A tightly coupled WASM accelerator.
- A true random number generator (TRNG).
- 16 kB of RAM memory.
- And some general peripherals: UART, GPIO and JTAG.

The work consist in achiving the following tasks:

1. Chip bring-up and initial testing on a breadboard.
2. Verification of all functionalities, including the accelerator and the TRNG.
3. Development of a dedicated PCB testing platform.

For this tasks to be achieved we expect a student with minimum experience manipulating tools like oscilloscopes, logic analyzer and knoledge of C code. 

If this sounds interesting to you and you	Tightly Coupled want to learn more on the world of chip design and testing, please reach out !
